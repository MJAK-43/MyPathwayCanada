FAQ

Common questions before users move deeper into the journey

Where should I begin if I am still a student?

Start with the Start Here page, then identify your current stage: study permit, PGWP, or PR planning.

Does this platform replace a lawyer or consultant?

No. The platform is designed as an educational and practical editorial hub, not as a substitute for legal advice.

How do I choose between Express Entry and BC PNP?

The site is structured to compare options based on timing, profile, province, and readiness level.

Will the content also be available in French?

Yes. The architecture is already prepared for a bilingual experience with English as default and French as a second layer.

Can I use this site before I even arrive in Canada?

Yes. Part of the guidance is meant to orient you before arrival and help you anticipate future transitions.
